The art of public voice

Civility
is a skill.
Practice it.

The Civil Tongue competition invites Behrend students to speak persuasively about timely, difficult questions while demonstrating evidence, listening, and respect. It makes the work of rhetorical education visible.

Real audiences. Difficult questions. A voice worth hearing.

William “Bill” McLean brings expertise in speech, persuasion, rhetoric, and public culture to this part of the department’s identity. The Civil Tongue competition gives students a public stage on which to turn careful argument into practiced communication.

Our story, our purpose

We do not just study communication.
We create it, practice it, research it, and shape it.

What makes Behrend different is not simply the list of things students can study. It is the work they begin doing while they are here: the stories they publish, the programs they broadcast, the arguments they craft, the questions they investigate, and the people they reach.

Why we exist

Our mission

Our mission is to transform the study of communication and media into meaningful, real-world experience. We bring theory and practice together, providing students with distinctive opportunities to create, compete, broadcast, research, and engage with the communities and industries they aspire to serve.

From speech competitions and student-run radio and television to producing content for professional news outlets, conducting meaningful research, and participating in transformative internships, our students learn by doing work that matters.

We cultivate critical thinkers, ethical communicators, creative media professionals, and engaged citizens who graduate not only with knowledge, but with the skills, confidence, and experience to make an impact.

Where we're going

Our vision

To be a nationally recognized model of experiential communication and media education, where the boundaries between classroom learning and professional practice are continually reimagined.

We envision a department where every student has access to extraordinary opportunities to discover their voice, develop their talents, contribute to meaningful work, and shape the future of communication and media.

Our graduates will be distinguished not simply by what they have learned, but by what they have already accomplished.

Our defining commitmentReal experiences. Real audiences. Real impact.

We believe the most powerful education happens when students move beyond learning about communication and begin practicing it, creating it, questioning it, and contributing to the world through it.
